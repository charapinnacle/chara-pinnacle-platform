# Runbook: release checklist

Every NFR of `docs/phase-1/requirements.js`, the findings of the whole-product audit (security SA-01 to SA-20, quality and UX findings, traceability gaps G-01 to G-14, the manual criteria of G-08 in item 14) that need a person, and unit U55 (D80). The list is in priority order: a P0 item open for an environment stops its release; a P1 item must be closed before the first real user (launch) and is otherwise tracked; P2 is the watch of the first 30 days. Platform and Operations run it before each production release (a repeat release re-checks only the items marked "each release"); record for each item the date, the person, the evidence and the result in the release record, which the approver signs (NFR-O2). Where another runbook holds the exact values, this list names it rather than repeating them.

## P0: release blockers

1. **Security settings** (NFR-S2, S5, S6, S7; SA-01, SA-03, SA-08; G-01). All of `security-checklist.md` section 3 done in its order, and the go-live gate of its section 2 exits 0 on the production exports without `--allow-draft-legal` (draft legal texts are a P1 decision of CHARA, item 10). Evidence: the gate's output and the advisor screen. Each release: CI `security` job green (no high or critical advisory in production dependencies, no secret key outside `supabase/`).
2. **Database** (NFR-S1, S3, C1, C3, O2; SA-17, SA-20; G-04, G-05). The migration history of production is repaired and `npx supabase db push --dry-run` prints only this release's migrations (`deploy.md` section 1); the reference seeds are present (`select (select count(*) from billing.plans) as plans, (select count(*) from billing.plan_limits) as limits, (select count(*) from public.occupations) as occupations;` is not zero in any column); at least two named, active platform administrators (O6; the gate fails at zero only); the security advisor shows only the expected warnings (`security-checklist.md` item 11). Each release: CI `db` job green (pgTAP, `db lint`, the RLS meta-test and the forbidden-column test).
3. **Backups** (NFR-A2, NFR-S4; O2, O5). Point-in-time recovery is on with at least 7 days (`backup-and-restore.md` section 1); a restore drill into a new project passed within the last quarter and its record shows an RTO under 4 hours (sections 4 and 5); the nightly copy of `passport-documents` to the second EU location runs and its last night succeeded (section 6). O5 is open: without that copy, launch needs CHARA's written acceptance of the documents' RPO gap, recorded with the release. The audit archive bucket exists and one month was exported and read back with the restore key (`audit-log.md` section 5).
4. **Deployment** (NFR-O2, NFR-O3, NFR-M2). The release is a tag of `main` with every CI job green, including the smoke of the five journeys (`smoke-journeys.spec.ts`), the accessibility and header sweep, and the performance budgets (bundle step of the `web` job, `perf` project of `e2e`); it was deployed to staging first through `deploy.md` sections 1 to 7, then to production in the same order; `/api/health` answers the tag. Each release.
5. **Rollback** (NFR-O2). The previous web build of the environment is kept and starting it was rehearsed on staging (`deploy.md` section 8); the migrations of the release are additive, so the previous build runs on the new schema. Each release.
6. **Headers as served** (NFR-S5). On the production origin the curl checks of `deploy.md` section 6 pass for a public page and a private page; HTTPS works on the domain and its subdomains (HSTS is sent with `includeSubDomains`). Each release.

## P1: before launch

7. **Monitoring** (NFR-A1, NFR-O1; SA-07). An external uptime monitor in the EU calls `https://<app>/api/health` every minute and alerts operations after two failures; its monthly availability is the NFR-A1 figure (target 99.9 %). The log drain of the project and the web host's logs reach the log platform with the alert rule on the key `alert` (`security-checklist.md` item 7). A daily check of the scheduled jobs: `select j.jobname, d.status, count(*) from cron.job_run_details d join cron.job j using (jobid) where d.start_time > now() - interval '1 day' group by 1, 2 order by 1, 2;` shows no `failed` row; the queues of `account-ops` and `notify` and the billing events have their own checks (`platform-staff.md` section 3, `transactional-emails.md`, `webhook-processing.md`).
8. **Error tracking** (NFR-O1). Not built: the web tier and the Edge Functions write structured JSON error lines without personal data (`console.error("<what> failed", { code })`), which the log platform of item 7 collects and counts. A dedicated error-tracking service needs a provider with EU storage and a dependency approval (OPEN_QUESTIONS.md O11); until then, the log platform's rule on `failed` lines per hour is the error alert.
9. **Mobile responsiveness and accessibility** (NFR-U1, NFR-U2). CI covers every page at 1280 and 360 px with axe (serious and critical violations) and the absence of horizontal scrolling, and the four main journeys by keyboard alone (`accessibility-sweep.spec.ts`, `keyboard-journeys.spec.ts`). By hand, because no test covers it: the five smoke journeys on one current iPhone (Safari) and one Android phone (Chrome), touch only, portrait and landscape; and the manual WCAG 2.2 AA audit of the public and candidate pages named by NFR-U1 (screen reader, 200 % and 400 % zoom and reflow, forced colours), each finding a ticket with an owner.
10. **Legal** (NFR-C1, NFR-C2; L1, L5, L7, L9). The legal texts that the go-live gate lists as drafts are approved by counsel and published; the legal-entity and privacy contact settings are filled (FR-H1 AC12, the gate checks them).
11. **Performance and capacity** (NFR-P1, NFR-P2). The load tests before launch on staging with 10,000 open vacancies and 50,000 candidates: search (`public-search.md`, FR-C3 AC11) and the employer dashboard (FR-E5) at the 95th percentile of 500 ms server time. The CI budgets (`apps/web/tests/perf-budget.json`) are a regression smoke on the local stack and do not replace them. Capacity: the web tier is stateless (`NEXT_SERVER_ACTIONS_ENCRYPTION_KEY` fixed at build time and the same for every build of the environment, `deploy.md` section 6), so a second instance is a host setting.
12. **Money rules against Stripe** (G-02; O7). The manual criteria FR-G2 AC12 (Stripe Tax) and FR-G4 AC12 (test clock: trial end, failed payment, cancellation after 7 days) pass in Stripe test mode, and the plans are mirrored (`checkout.md` section 3).
13. **Email** (FR-I1, FR-A1; W1). A confirmation, a reset, an invitation and a status email reach an external mailbox from the production domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC passing (`account-emails.md` section 3, `deploy.md` section 7).
14. **Manual criteria with a recorded result** (G-08). Each one is done once before launch and then at its own interval; record the date, the person and the evidence named here.
    - FR-I1 AC13, credential rotation: the Auth SMTP password and the `notify` key are rotated through `account-emails.md` section 5 at the interval of `key-rotation.md`; evidence: a test email delivered through each path and the old keys shown as revoked in Resend. The other credentials follow `key-rotation.md`.
    - FR-F2 AC12, quarterly sample review: the privacy contact named in `private.settings` reviews at least 25 administrative audit rows of the quarter (`audit-log.md` section 6); evidence: the review record with each row's result.
    - FR-A8 AC12, consent-ledger export: no procedure exists yet. The legal versions are exported from the console (`legal-documents.md` section 1, "Export all versions"), but the export query of the consent ledger that the criterion asks for is not written; until it is, launch needs the privacy contact's written acceptance, recorded here.
    - FR-C7 AC12, appeal decided within 7 days: the appeals log of `vacancy-moderation.md` sections 1 and 2 exists and is kept by the Trust & Safety Administrator; evidence: the log and its monthly "decision within 7 days" figure.
    - FR-A9 AC8, legal review of the age wording: part of item 10; counsel reviews the `age_18_plus` text for each launch country; evidence: the reviewer, the countries and the date, and a new published version when the wording changed.

## P2: the first 30 days

15. **Daily for two weeks, then weekly**: uptime and error alerts of items 7 and 8; `private.security_events` by kind (`security-checklist.md` item 7); the queued and abandoned jobs of `account-ops` and `notify`; billing events in `error`; the daily quota refusals (`quota_refused` log lines); the cron check of item 7. Each finding is a ticket; a data incident follows the GDPR 72-hour process (ARCHITECTURE.md section 12).
16. **Weekly**: the security and performance advisors of the project (new warnings are tickets); `npm audit` and the Dependabot pull requests; the reconciliation of candidate files (`platform-staff.md` section 3).
17. **After 30 days**: the KPIs of the SOP runbooks read for the first time with real data (each runbook's KPI section); the bundle budgets of `perf-budget.json` reviewed against the build (lower them when the pages became smaller, so that the 10 % headroom stays a regression guard); the first quarterly restore drill and audit-archive restore test scheduled (`backup-and-restore.md` section 2).
