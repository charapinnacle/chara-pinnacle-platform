# Runbook: switching release 2a on (hosted)

Release 2a (partner accounts and discovery, units V01 to V31, V77, V78, V81 of `docs/phase-2/BUILD_ORDER.md`) is built behind the setting `release_partner_directories`, off by default. This runbook is the checklist for turning it on in a hosted environment: staging first, then production. Platform and Operations run it; record for each item the date, the person, the evidence and the result in the release record, which the approver signs. An item marked **stop** that is open stops the switch. The general release checklist (`release-checklist.md`) and the security checklist (`security-checklist.md`) apply as for every release; this list adds what release 2a needs.

## 1. Prerequisites outside the code (stop)

1. Counsel has approved the partner legal documents (Partner Terms, Recruitment Company Terms, Staffing Company Terms, the employer-pays commitment) and they are published from the console (`legal-documents.md`); the settings editor refuses the release switch while one of them is a draft, and the gate of section 4 fails.
2. The payment provider is live with the six paid partner prices mirrored (`checkout.md`, `scripts/sync-stripe-plans.mjs`) and a customer-portal configuration for each partner type set as the Edge Function secrets `STRIPE_PORTAL_CONFIGURATION_RECRUITMENT_COMPANY` and `STRIPE_PORTAL_CONFIGURATION_STAFFING_COMPANY`.
3. The database plan includes point-in-time recovery, the web host is on a commercial plan, and the sending domain passes SPF, DKIM and DMARC (`deploy.md` section 7).
4. Trust & Safety staff are named and hold the role `trust_safety` with two-step verification: **at least two people**, because an appeal is decided by a moderator who did not take the decision (V28; with one person no appeal can be decided). The Verification Reviewer role is granted to whoever reads the partner reviews without deciding (`platform-staff.md`).

## 2. Deployment

5. Every unit of release 2a is merged into `main` and the release is a tag of `main` with every CI job green, including the `release-2a-journey` project of the `e2e` job (sign-up to public profile, NFR-O4) and pgTAP `155_find_partners_load` of the `db` job (NFR-P3).
6. The migrations are pushed (`deploy.md` section 1) and every Edge Function is redeployed from the tag (`deploy.md` section 2): release 2a changed `account-ops`, `document-url`, `scan-document`, `billing-checkout`, `billing-webhook` (shared billing code) and `notify` (shared code), and added `billing-plan-price`.
7. The scheduled jobs of release 2a exist and ran on their last day without a `failed` row: `partner-licence-expiry`, `workforce-availability-confirm`, `aggregate-org-analytics`, `follower-digest`, `snapshot-partner-statuses` (`select jobname, schedule from cron.job order by 1;` and the daily check of `release-checklist.md` item 7).
8. Realtime private channels: in the project's Realtime settings, public channel access is turned off so that only private channels (with their policies on `realtime.messages`) are served. The bell of the in-app notifications (V02) listens on the private channel `user:<id>`. Confirm on staging: a notification queued for a signed-in test user appears in the bell without a reload.

## 3. Data and settings (stop)

9. **Acceptances outside the Commitments step.** Before the hardening of V08, a user could accept an organisation-level document (the employer-pays commitment, the partner DPA) for themselves. Such a row would count for any partner organisation that user later owns. Count them with the query of the `stray_organization_consents` key in `security-checklist.md` section 2 (the gate of section 4 runs it); it must be 0. If it is not, cancel each row with a ticketed, audited statement of the database owner that adds a withdrawal after it (the ledger is append-only; nothing is deleted): `insert into public.consents (user_id, purpose, version, action) select user_id, purpose, version, 'withdrawn' from public.consents where id in (<the ids the query lists>);`, then run the count again.
10. **Settings** reviewed in the settings editor (`/en/admin/settings`, audited): `entitlements_enforced` (decide whether plan limits are enforced from the first day of release 2a; while it is off every plan gate is open to every organisation and the ranking page describes the enforced state), `partner_review_target_working_days`, `licence_number_patterns`, `licence_valid_until_max_years`, `licence_expiry_reminder_days`, `workforce_listing_stale_days`, `workforce_confirm_reminder_days`, the `rate_limit_partner_*` limits, and in the retention editor the release 2a rows. `release_data_export` is a switch of its own (FR-S3, V29) that needs no legal document: it may be turned on before or with release 2a; record the decision.
11. **Approved texts.** The client approves the partner network copy of the public pages (`apps/web/lib/public/partner-network.ts`, placeholder text until then) and the client or counsel reviews the wording of the ranking page `/[lang]/legal/ranking` (`apps/web/lib/partners/ranking.ts`, NFR-C8).
12. **Open decisions recorded.** The privacy adviser has decided on the residual re-identification risk of the profile view analytics (V20), and the analytics or referrer decision for the KPIs that cannot be measured yet is recorded (`OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` P25). The open points of the NFR-C7 review (`docs/phase-2/NFR-C7_REVIEW.md`), above all the complaint route of reporters (L12) and of declined partners, are decided by counsel or accepted in writing by the client.

## 4. Checks before the switch (stop)

13. **Go-live gate.** Export the three files of `security-checklist.md` section 2 from the environment, set `release_partner_directories` to `true` in your copy of `settings.json` (the export itself still says false), and run `npm run go-live:check -- settings.json plans.json release.json`. It must exit 0: no Phase 2 line, `legal_release_gaps` empty for `partner_accounts`, the partner plans mirrored, the portal secrets set, `stray_organization_consents` 0. The line `Release switches on:` names what goes live. Delete the files afterwards.
14. **Performance (NFR-P3).** The database check passes on a staging copy: `DATABASE_URL=<staging> node scripts/load/find-partners.mjs` (exit 0, every search under 100 ms at the 95th percentile; never against production: it holds locks until its rollback). The HTTP check of `docs/perf/release-2a.md` (section "Operational check at the HTTP level") passes on staging: 95 % of the directory and profile pages under 500 ms to the first byte with 5,000 listed partners.
15. **Accessibility (NFR-U3).** The manual audit of `release-2a-accessibility-audit.md` is done on staging and has no open blocking finding.
16. **Rehearsal on staging.** With release 2a on in staging, a person walks the journey that CI automates (`apps/web/tests/e2e/release-2a-journey.spec.ts`) with a real mailbox: sign-up as a recruitment company, confirmation email, onboarding with the commitment, two-step verification, a licence and a listing, submission; a Verification Reviewer reads the review, Trust & Safety list the partner with a reason; the partner receives the email "listed", appears in Find Partners for its destination and occupation, and its profile shows nothing private (legal name, licence number, email).

## 5. The switch

17. A platform administrator turns `release_partner_directories` on in the settings editor (Releases; at aal2, audited as a settings change). The partner sign-up choices, the onboarding steps, the directories, the profiles, the partner review queue, the bell, appeals and the partner pages of the console appear at once (the switch is read per request).
18. Smoke within the hour: the public pages show the partner sections; `/en/partners/recruitment` and `/en/partners/staffing` answer 200 (empty is expected on the first day); the sitemap lists the partner profiles once one is listed; a test sign-up reaches the Company step; the console shows Partner reviews and Appeals to Trust & Safety.
19. **Rollback.** Turning the switch off hides every release 2a page and RPC again (they answer not found) and keeps every row; partners who signed up meanwhile keep their accounts and return to their state when the switch is on again. Decide with the client what to tell them.

## 6. Note for developers

The release switch is one row for the whole database, so the end-to-end specs that set it run as projects of their own, one after the other (`apps/web/playwright.config.ts`). Never run two partner projects together with `--no-deps`: they would switch the release on and off under each other. After a local run, check that `select key, value from private.settings where key like 'release_%';` shows every switch false.
