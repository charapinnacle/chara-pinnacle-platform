# NFR-C7 review: partner approval, moderation decisions and appeals (release 2a)

V78, 27 October 2026. NFR-C7 (business user traceability and moderation): partners are business users, whose legal name, registration number and country are collected and checked before listing; every moderation decision follows notice and action with a statement of reasons. This review reads the flows as built in V11 (partner approval, FR-J4), V27 (moderation decisions, FR-S1) and V28 (appeals, FR-S1) against six questions: statement of reasons, second-person review, audit, notices, timelines and redress. It names the code and the tests that show each answer, what this unit fixed, and the findings that need a decision or a later unit. It is an engineering review, not legal advice: the legal reading (EU Digital Services Act Art. 16, 17, 20; the platform-to-business Regulation (EU) 2019/1150 Art. 4 and 11 for partners as business users) is counsel's, through the open questions named below.

## 1. Partner approval (V11, FR-J4)

| Question | As built | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Identity collected before listing | The Company step requires the legal name, the country where the company is based and a registration number, VAT number or other identifier with its kind; `create_organization` refuses a partner without them and `submit_partner_for_review` names the first missing field (`private.partner_profile_missing`). The identifier is normalised and links to organisations with the same identifier are flagged for staff (`private.organization_identifier_links`). | pgTAP `121`, `127`, `129`; e2e `partner-registration.spec.ts` (AC12), `release-2a-journey.spec.ts` |
| Checked before listing | Only `admin_decide_partner` with the decision approve sets `listed_at`; every directory, profile and search reads `listed_at` with the review and moderation state (`private.is_listed_partner`, `search_partners`). The review page shows the company details, the identifier links, the declared licences with their evidence (opened through the staff broker, logged in `audit.partner_document_access_log`) and a checklist (legal entity, website, licences). | pgTAP `129`, `142`, `155` (no unlisted partner in the results at 5,000 partners); e2e `partner-approval.spec.ts`, `release-2a-journey.spec.ts` (404 before the decision, listed after) |
| Statement of reasons | Every decision (approve, request information, decline) needs a reason of 10 to 2,000 characters (`private.statement_of_reasons`), stored in the append-only `public.partner_review_decisions`. | pgTAP `129`; e2e `partner-approval.spec.ts` (empty reason refused, focus on the field) |
| Second-person review | One Trust & Safety administrator decides. The Verification Reviewer reads the review and opens the evidence but cannot decide. A member of the partner, the holder of an invitation to it, or a member of an organisation linked to it by identifier is refused (`own_organization`). There is no second review of a decline (finding C7-1). | pgTAP `129`; e2e `partner-approval.spec.ts` (read-only reviewer) |
| Audit | One `partner.review_decided` row per decision with the decision and the reason, beside the decision row (`decided_by`, `submitted_at`, from and to state); `partner.submitted_for_review` for each submission. Audit rows are kept six years. | pgTAP `127`, `129` |
| Notices | `partner_approved`, `partner_info_requested`, `partner_declined` are queued in the same transaction as the decision to the owners and admins, mandatory, with the reason for a request for information and a decline, never the staff member (V14). The dashboard shows the outcome and the reason. | pgTAP `132`; Vitest `partner-emails`, `partner-dashboard`; e2e `partner-review-emails.spec.ts`, `release-2a-journey.spec.ts` |
| Timelines | Target `partner_review_target_working_days` (1, setting) shown to the partner on Ready and to staff as the due time and "Overdue" in the queue (oldest first); not enforced. | Redress | A request for information reopens a step and the partner submits again. The decline email names the Complaints and Dispute Process page as the appeal route. **Fixed in V78:** the dashboard of a declined partner linked to the Contact page instead; it now links to the same Complaints and Dispute Process page ("How to appeal this decision"). | Vitest `partner-dashboard`; e2e `partner-approval.spec.ts` |

## 2. Moderation decisions (V27, FR-S1)

| Question | As built | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Notice | `submit_report` takes reports of a partner profile (fee demand first) from visitors and signed-in users, with a category, a text of 20 to 2,000 characters, a good-faith statement and a reference; the queue puts fee demands first. | pgTAP `147`, `148`; e2e `partner-profile.spec.ts`, `reports-queue.spec.ts` |
| Statement of reasons | `moderation_decide` (hide, suspend, dismiss) needs a statement of 10 to 2,000 characters, stored in `moderation_actions.statement_of_reasons` and shown to the affected organisation on its workforce profile, its appeals page and in the notice. The statement is free text: it has no structured legal or contractual ground and does not say that no automated means were used (finding C7-3). | pgTAP `148`; Vitest `moderation-emails` |
| Second-person review | Trust & Safety at aal2 decide alone; a member of the organisation is refused. The second person is the appeal (section 3). | pgTAP `148` |
| Audit | Two rows per decision on a report: the action on the target (`partner_profile.hide`, `organization.suspend`, ... with the reasons and the report) and the move of the report (`report.actioned`, `report.dismissed` with the decision); a race between two moderators is refused and writes nothing. | pgTAP `148`; e2e `reports-queue.spec.ts` |
| Notices | The owners and admins of the organisation receive `moderation_decision` (or the Phase 1 `account_suspended`, `vacancy_hidden`) with the reasons and the appeal link, mandatory, queued in the same transaction. A signed-in reporter receives the outcome and the reference, never the reasons or the target; a dismissed reporter is pointed to the Complaints and Dispute Process. A visitor reporter gets no email (their address is not verified; they read nothing, P23). | pgTAP `148`; Vitest `moderation-emails`, `notifications-present`; e2e `in-app-notifications.spec.ts` |
| Timelines | `report_fee_demand_target_working_days` (1), `report_first_look_target_working_days` (2), `report_decision_target_days` (7): settings, fixed on each report as its due time, shown in the queue, not enforced. | pgTAP `147`, `148` |
| Redress | The appeal of V28 for a hidden profile, a hidden vacancy and a suspended organisation. Not for a dismissal (the reporter, finding C7-2) or for the Phase 1 suspension of a user account. | section 3 |

## 3. Appeals (V28, FR-S1)

| Question | As built | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Who and when | The owner or an admin of the affected organisation at aal2, once per decision, while the decision is in effect and within `appeal_window_months` (6, setting) of it; grounds of 20 to 2,000 characters. | pgTAP `149` |
| Second-person review | `decide_appeal` refuses the moderator who took the decision (`same_reviewer`) and a member of the organisation (`own_organization`); the console tells the decider that the appeal is someone else's. With a single Trust & Safety administrator no appeal can be decided: two named people are a go-live condition (`docs/runbooks/release-2a-go-live.md` item 4). | pgTAP `149`; e2e `partner-profile.spec.ts` |
| Statement of reasons | Upholding and reversing both need reasons, sent to the appellant and shown on the appeal page. | pgTAP `149`; Vitest `appeal-view` |
| Audit | `appeal.lodged`, `appeal.upheld` or `appeal.restored` (with the reversal's own moderation row); the reversal rate is stored (`appeals.reversal_action_id`). Decided appeals are kept 365 days after the decision and never before the longest window has passed. | pgTAP `149` |
| Notices | One `moderation_decision` to the appellant (or to the current owners and admins if the appellant left), with the outcome and reasons; a restored suspension also sends `account_reinstated`. | pgTAP `149`; Vitest `moderation-emails` |
| Timelines | `appeal_decision_target_days` (7, setting), fixed as the due time, shown, not enforced; the console lists open appeals oldest first. CHARA still has to confirm the 6 months and 7 days (decision 7.2). | pgTAP `149` |

## 4. Findings

| # | Finding | Severity | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| C7-1 | A declined partner has no complaint route in the product and no second review: the decision is final and the appeals of V28 cover moderation decisions only. The email and (since V78) the dashboard point to the Complaints and Dispute Process page, which is still a draft placeholder. Partners are business users; whether the platform-to-business Regulation's internal complaint-handling (Art. 11) or the DSA's (Art. 20) applies to a refused listing is for counsel. | high before go-live | Counsel decides (new open question L13, `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`); if a route in the product is required, a follow-up unit that extends `submit_appeal`/`decide_appeal` to `partner_review_decisions` (decline only) before release 2a goes live. Until then Trust & Safety handle such complaints by email with a second person (operational). |
| C7-2 | L12: a reporter cannot contest the dismissal of a report in the product (DSA Art. 20(1) names the notice submitter); they are pointed to the Complaints and Dispute Process. Worker appeals on messages wait for V73. | high before go-live | Counsel (L12, decision 6.4). If required: a follow-up unit after V78 that opens `submit_appeal` to the signed-in reporter of a dismissed report; worker appeals: V73. |
| C7-3 | The statement of reasons is free text. It does not record the ground (illegal content, with the law, or incompatibility with the terms, with the clause) or say that the decision was taken by a person without automated means, which DSA Art. 17(3) lists; nothing is sent to the DSA transparency database (Art. 24(5)). | medium | Counsel (L10) on which fields are required and whether CHARA is exempt as a micro or small enterprise; then a follow-up unit (a ground field on `moderation_actions` and the notices). Interim (operational): the moderation policy asks Trust & Safety to name the rule broken in each statement. |
| C7-4 | "Checked before listing" leaves no structured record of what was checked: the checklist of the review page (legal entity against the register, website, licences) is shown, not stored; only the reason text records it. | low | Operational: the Trust & Safety procedure asks the reviewer to name the register and checks in the reason. Storing the checklist would be a follow-up unit (no build order unit owns it). |
| C7-5 | A listed partner that changes its legal name or identifier stays listed during re-review (V03 design: the public profile shows the display name, never the legal name or identifier). | low, accepted | Trust & Safety: re-reviews come first in the queue by submission time with the same 1-working-day target; recorded as accepted risk. |
| C7-6 | Every redress link (decline email and dashboard, dismissed reporter, notices without a decision id) leads to the Complaints and Dispute Process page, whose published text is a draft placeholder. | high before go-live | Counsel and CHARA: the approved text is a prerequisite of release 2a (`release-2a-go-live.md` item 12; the Phase 1 go-live gate already fails on a draft). |
| C7-7 | Notices of a hide or a suspension link to the appeal but do not state the appeal deadline; the appeals page does ("you can appeal until ..."). | low | V57, which extends the moderation notices to the job order targets, adds the deadline to the payload and the text. |

## 5. Departures carried from V27 and V28

Recorded in their coverage files and kept as they are; none weakens NFR-C7:

- V27: suspension notices stay the Phase 1 `account_suspended` and `vacancy_hidden` (with the reasons and the appeal route); a decision on a report writes two audit rows, not one; a nine-character statement is refused before the table check; the decision target is fixed when the report is stored, shown and not enforced; the appeal route was the Complaints and Dispute Process page until V28; hidden partners' geographies, offer and listings are no longer readable by other users; the staff reading of every listing is for Trust & Safety and the Platform Administrator, while the Verification Reviewer keeps the partner review page.
- V28: the outcome `restore` is stored as `restored` and shown as "Decision reversed"; `appeals.organization_id` is added; a restore writes the appeal row and the reversal's moderation row; one notice to the appellant (or the current owners and admins); appealable decisions are the hide of a partner profile or a vacancy and the suspension of an organisation (not a dismissal, not a user suspension, not a message decision: C7-2); no rate limit beyond one appeal per decision; every appeal page and RPC is behind release 2a; the 7-day target is shown, not enforced, and no staff notice is sent on a new appeal; appeal references `A-` and six digits in the console only.
